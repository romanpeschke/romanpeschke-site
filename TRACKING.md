## GTM contents (as of Version 5)

- GA4 Google Tag and Google Ads Google Tag, all pages.
- Conversion Linker with cross-domain linking to skool.com, so a Google ad click survives the hop from romanpeschke.com to Skool (visible as `_gl=` on outbound Skool links).
- Meta Pixel tag (CAPI template), fires on DOM ready. It was added from the Meta side, not by hand.
- Trigger "Click - Skool link": any link click whose URL contains `skool.com`.
- Tag "GA4 - Event - skool_cta_click", with parameters `link_url` and `link_text` (built-in Click URL and Click Text variables).

## GA4 settings

- Linked to Google Ads 950-129-5564 (personalized advertising and auto-tagging on).
- Event data retention 14 months. Google Signals on.
- `skool_cta_click` is a normal event, not a key event, on purpose: the real conversion is the Skool signup.
- `link_text` and `link_url` are **not** registered as custom dimensions, so they only appear in raw event data. Register them in GA4 Admin, Custom definitions, to see them in reports (data starts from the day they are added).
- No explorations, audiences, or filters exist yet.

## The funnel

1. Site visit: GA4 pageview, Meta Pixel PageView, PostHog pageview.
2. Click on a Skool CTA on the site: GA4 `skool_cta_click`.
3. On skool.com: Skool's own Google Ads plugin (AW tag plus conversion label, set up in Skool) fires the about-page view (retargeting) and the signup (conversion). This reports to Google Ads, and reaches GA4 through the Ads link.

## Performance rules (do not undo)

Tracker scripts are about 1 MB. Loaded normally, they took mobile PageSpeed from about 90 down to 61 (LCP 9.4 s). On 2026-09-29 they were changed so they never block the page:

- **`afterLoad` helper.** The first script in `<head>` defines `afterLoad(fn)`, which runs `fn` once the page has loaded **and** painted its first content, then waits for browser idle (3 s fallback if nothing paints, for example in a background tab). GTM and the PostHog snippet are both wrapped in it. Do not replace this with Google's stock GTM snippet.
- **PostHog** runs with `disable_session_recording: true` and `disable_surveys: true`. Neither feature is used.
- **Fonts** are self-hosted in `assets/fonts/` with a one-year immutable cache (`vercel.json`). No Google Fonts links.

Trade-off: trackers start about a second after the page appears. Visitors who leave, or tap a Skool CTA, in that first second are not counted, and a Google ad click in that window loses its Skool attribution. This was accepted for the speed gain.

Result: mobile PageSpeed 92, desktop 100, SEO 100. What remains in PageSpeed's list (unused JavaScript, long tasks, Meta's 20-minute cache) comes from the tags themselves and cannot be fixed without removing tracking.
