# Guide page template (Read mode)

- **Job and audience:** a business owner arrives from an Instagram DM link on a phone, mid-scroll, wanting the one thing the Reel promised. Read mode. They should be able to do the thing without reading anything twice.
- **Outcome and proof:** the guide works end to end; the Skool offer (top card and footer) is seen but never blocks the steps. Proof is Roman's own language and the actual URLs, commands, and screenshots. No invented claims.
- **Selected direction:** Monochrome with a dark opening band. Near-black band holds title, subtitle, and tool chips edge to edge; the page returns to near-white for everything that must be read. One indigo accent, used only where the reader needs a pointer (step numerals, focus, offer label). Figtree throughout. The memorable moment is the switch from black to white as you scroll into the work. Approved comp: previews/mono-hero/index.html (gitignored preview the user picked on 2026-09-05).
- **Scope:** shared stylesheet at /assets/guide.css; guides/claude-meta-mcp and guides/claude-code-tokens rebuilt as exemplars; homepage becomes a plain chronological index (newest first) in the same world. Remaining 14 guides migrate after approval. Untouched: copy, URLs, SEO head, structured data, PostHog.
- **Ranges and states:** guides run from 5 steps to 15 tips with grouped phases, comparisons, and example blocks; single or many tool chips; with or without FAQ. Hover, focus, selection, reduced motion all themed.
- **Constraints:** static HTML, no build step, Google Fonts only, mobile first. No eyebrow labels, no emoji icons, no Reel sign-off anywhere. The offer card copy stays exactly as written.
