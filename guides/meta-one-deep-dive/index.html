Meta is now selling organic reach. Meta One is Meta Verified on steroids: four business tiers (Essential, Advanced, Expert, Max), each priced per Instagram profile, Facebook Page, or WhatsApp Business number, that let you pay for links in your content, data on people who don't follow you, ranking in search, and a human at Meta. Here's what's in each tier and how to decide.

How the tiers work

The name is misleading. "Meta One" sounds like one plan. It's 4 plans, each bought per platform, so prices are per profile per plan per month.

Essential, $14.99 per profile

Meta Verified with a few additions.

  1. 1

    Verified badge

    On Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp Business, once your profile passes Meta's verification check. If you sell anything expertise-based, a product, or a service, the badge builds trust and your ads might perform a bit better because of it.

  2. 2

    Impersonation protection

    Proactive monitoring on Instagram and Facebook for fake accounts pretending to be you.

  3. 3

    Enhanced linksheet and link page

    Images next to the links in your Facebook profile linksheet, and a shareable link page in your Instagram bio with up to 20 links.

  4. 4

    More Meta Business Agent access

    An AI agent that answers customers 24/7 on WhatsApp Business. Essential's usage allowance is small; running it at scale means a higher tier.

  5. 5

    Creator assistant, more Meta AI, and the Plus bundles

    Creator assistant on Facebook gives you AI-generated insights about your content performance, audience, and monetization. Creators get more Meta AI image and video generation. Everyone gets the Instagram Plus, Facebook Plus, and WhatsApp Plus perks (custom app icons, story previews, messaging extras), one verified WhatsApp channel, and a custom searchable web URL for your business.

Advanced, $49.99 per profile

Everything in Essential, plus:

  1. 1

    Links in posts and reels

    Clickable links straight from your organic content: up to 4 Instagram posts, 4 Instagram reels, 8 Facebook posts, and 8 Facebook reels a month.

    • New content only. You can't add a link to a post that's already live.
    • Links can't be edited after you share.
    • Unused links don't roll over. Archive a post and the credit is gone.
    • Links to Meta apps, catalog product tags, and links in ads don't count against the limit.
  2. 2

    Advanced insights

    • Competitive insights (Instagram). Add up to 10 public accounts in your space and compare net followers, reels, posts, stories, and ads over up to six months.
    • Custom audience insights (Facebook). Data on people who see and interact with your content but don't follow you yet, plus your most active followers and newest fans.
    • Extended history and export (Instagram). Look back beyond 90 days and export content-level insights for reels, posts, and stories as a CSV, which you can feed to an AI to find which content produces customers.
  3. 3

    Paid organic growth

    • Optimized search (Instagram and Facebook). Your profile ranks higher in search results.
    • Featured in Feed (Facebook). Your profile gets placed in a dedicated carousel in the feeds of people who may be interested in your business.
    • Bold follow button (Facebook). A more prominent follow button on your reels.
    • Follow invites (Facebook). People who engage with your content automatically get invited to follow you.
  4. 4

    Enhanced profile

    Add up to 3 locations, connect up to 3 other profiles, and show 20 mentions on Instagram.

  5. 5

    Scheduling

    Schedule posts and reels up to a year out and stories 30 days out, with suggested times based on when your followers are active, grid preview, and bulk upload on instagram.com. On Facebook, auto-post when your audience is most active.

  6. 6

    Shared Instagram access

    Give up to 4 people access to post, reply to comments, and manage the account without sharing your password.

  7. 7

    Human support

    One support case a month with a real person.

  8. 8

    WhatsApp Business: broadcasts, channels, devices

    500 business broadcast credits a month, 3 verified channels, 6 linked devices. Broadcasts send promotions to many customers at once, scheduled, with performance tracking.

  9. 9

    Original content credit and more AI

    Two requests a month to get notified when others use your content and to request a credit label on Instagram. Plus more Business Agent and Meta AI usage than Essential.

Which one to get

If you rely on Meta for acquiring customers, start with Advanced on your most engaged platform(s). You get every feature the higher tiers have, in reasonable quantities.

At a minimum, I'd get Essential on FB and IG. For $30/mo you can instantly boost ad performance and brand trust.

On the fence? Run a test

Get Advanced for 30-90 days, use the links feature, create unique links for attribution, and track any additional revenue they generate.

If your organic links produce more revenue than the cost of the tier, keep it. If they produce more revenue than the cost of a higher tier, upgrade. If they produce nothing, downgrade.

Before you buy

  • Don't buy Essential expecting the reach features. Links in content, advanced insights, search ranking, and follow invites all start at Advanced.
  • Don't assume one subscription covers all your profiles. It's per Instagram profile, Facebook Page, or WhatsApp number. Three profiles on Advanced is about $150 a month.
  • Don't waste link credits. They reset on your renewal date, can't be added to old posts, can't be edited, and don't come back if you archive the post.
  • Don't expect the growth tools on Instagram yet. Featured in Feed, bold follow button, follow invites, and custom audience insights are Facebook-only today.

Get Meta One

Plans, pricing, and the subscribe button are on Meta One for Business. You subscribe from the Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Business, or Meta AI app, or from Meta Business Suite on the web if you already have a business profile, and the benefits list you see during onboarding is the one that applies to your account.

FAQ

  • Does one Meta One subscription cover Instagram, Facebook, and WhatsApp?

    No. Business plans are priced per Facebook Page, Instagram profile, or WhatsApp Business number. Some benefits like the Plus app perks and AI usage can apply across profiles linked in the same Accounts Center, but the business features apply to the subscribed profile.

  • Which tier includes links in posts and reels?

    Advanced and above. Advanced gives you 4 Instagram posts, 4 Instagram reels, and 8 Facebook posts and reels a month. Expert raises that to 8 and 20, Max to 12 and unlimited on Facebook. Essential has none.

  • Is Meta Verified going away?

    Meta says it's bringing the best of Meta Verified into Meta One. If you're on Meta Verified now, nothing changes yet. Meta will notify you when you can switch.

  • Can I cancel anytime?

    Yes. You keep your benefits through the end of the billing period. It renews automatically and bills through the App Store or Google Play, and you manage it on the same platform and device where you bought it.
